Play a round with friends or take on a solo challenge. MoD-IT is a collection of browser games made by Grzegorz Byrka: familiar board games, a card game of predictions, and original driving and exploration games. No account, subscription or download is needed.

Play with friends

Create a room, share its invitation and wait for your friends to join. Keep the host's tab open throughout the game. Multiplayer needs JavaScript and a browser that supports WebRTC; there is no public matchmaking or registration.

  • CHESSClassic chess for two, with legal move hints and an optional clock. Mouse and touch.
  • CHECKERSTwo-player checkers with compulsory captures and flying kings. Mouse and touch.
  • Catana CodexBuild settlements, trade resources and develop an island with 2–4 players. Mouse and touch.
  • PLANPredict how many tricks you will take in a card game for 2–6 players. Mouse and touch.
  • TOWRace friends with a trailer and navigate a winding road. 2–4 drivers; keyboard required.
  • HAMSTER multiplayerExplore a shared habitat and collect treats in a three-minute competition. Keyboard and touch.

A challenge of your own

These solo games use keyboard controls. Each game includes its instructions and starts when you choose to play.

  • DOCKGuide a container ship into its berth. Manage the engine and rudder, or call on a tugboat.
  • PARKPark carefully against the clock, then try the added challenge of maneuvering a trailer.
  • HAMSTERExplore a three-level hamster habitat, find treats and run in the wheel.

About MoD-IT Games

This is an independent collection by Grzegorz Byrka. Games run in your browser. Solo progress and preferences can be stored on your device; multiplayer rooms use browser-to-browser connections. The privacy policy explains hosting, local storage, multiplayer connections, analytics and advertising.

The game pages describe which controls are supported. Keyboard-only games are clearly marked; choose a mouse-and-touch game when playing on a phone or tablet.
